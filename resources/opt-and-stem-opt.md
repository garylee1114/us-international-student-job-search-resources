# OPT and STEM OPT: a verification checklist

This page is a planning aid. It does not calculate an individual deadline or determine eligibility.

## Post-completion OPT

1. Confirm your program end date and the school's process with your DSO. Do not substitute a graduation ceremony date for the SEVIS/I-20 date.
2. Use the current [USCIS Form I-765 Instructions](https://www.uscis.gov/sites/default/files/document/forms/i-765instr.pdf) and your DSO's written guidance to check the filing sequence and your individual dates.
3. Keep a personal record of employment reporting, authorization dates, and any periods of unemployment. Ask the DSO before relying on an online summary.

## STEM OPT extension

STEM OPT is a separate extension workflow, not merely an extra date added to post-completion OPT.

1. Start with USCIS's current [Form I-765 Instructions](https://www.uscis.gov/sites/default/files/document/forms/i-765instr.pdf). The current instructions describe filing up to 90 days before the current OPT expires, and require an I-20 endorsed by the DSO within 60 days before filing.
2. Confirm the degree, E-Verify employer information, training-plan requirements, and your DSO's process. The employer name and E-Verify number used for the filing must be confirmed with the employer and school.
3. Treat each unemployment or reporting question as individual. Official resources distinguish the post-completion OPT and STEM OPT limits; a general timeline does not replace a DSO's review of your SEVIS record.
