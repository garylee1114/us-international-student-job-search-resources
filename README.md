## Practical job-posting check

Before investing time in a reposted U.S. job listing, record the official careers source, requisition ID, team and location, application status, dates, and what remains unverified. Read Landin Career's Google-indexed guide: [How to verify whether a U.S. job is still open](https://landincareer.com/insights/is-job-still-open/).

## Work authorization and sponsorship conversations

Before answering an application, keep three questions separate: your verified current work authorization, any future employer sponsorship needs, and a confirmed start date. Save the exact question and its time qualifiers; do not turn a pending application into an approval or use an employer's historical filings as a promise for a current role.

Landin Career's new Chinese-language guide includes a private fact-card structure and original English clarification examples: [How to discuss work authorization and sponsorship](https://landincareer.com/insights/opt-sponsorship-questions/). The examples must be adapted to verified facts; they do not determine personal eligibility or provide a universal Yes/No answer. Confirm personal authorization questions with your school's DSO or a qualified professional, and role policy with the recruiting team.

## Use these sources carefully

- A prior LCA or H-1B filing is historical information, not a promise that a current role sponsors a visa.
- An E-Verify search result is only one requirement relevant to STEM OPT. Confirm the job, employer, training plan, and personal eligibility with the employer and DSO.
- Use the official links in each resource at the time you make a decision; policies and tool interfaces can change.
